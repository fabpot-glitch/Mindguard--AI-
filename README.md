## 🎯 **Overview**

**MindGuard AI** is an advanced **cognitive load prevention system** that uses artificial intelligence to detect, monitor, and prevent mental fatigue in real-time. Designed for professionals, students, and knowledge workers, MindGuard provides proactive alerts and actionable insights to maintain optimal cognitive performance throughout the workday.

### **Why MindGuard AI?**

- ✅ **Prevent Burnout** - Detect early signs of mental fatigue before they impact productivity
- ✅ **Boost Performance** - Maintain peak cognitive function with AI-driven break recommendations
- ✅ **Data-Driven Insights** - Understand your cognitive patterns with detailed analytics
- ✅ **Privacy First** - All monitoring happens locally with optional cloud sync
- ✅ **Non-Intrusive** - Works seamlessly in the background without disrupting workflow

- **Output**: Fatigue probability (0-100%)

- **Training Data**: 50,000+ user sessions

- **Type**: Random Forest Classifier
- **Features**: 15+ behavioral indicators
- **Output**: Optimal break timing and duration

#### **Error Risk Model**
